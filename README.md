# UndaNova

The website for UndaNova, a Kenyan software studio that builds practical websites, apps and business systems for small and growing businesses, NGOs and schools.

*Unda* is Swahili for "create" and *nova* is Latin for "new": together, "create something new".

## What's on the site

- **Home**: who UndaNova is, what it builds, why clients choose it, featured work and how a project runs
- **Services**: business websites, NGO and school systems, e-commerce with M-Pesa, and support and maintenance, with ways to work together and answers to common questions
- **Work**: projects and case studies
- **SaaS**: ready-made NGO, POS and restaurant systems (coming soon)
- **About**: the founder's story and the meaning of the name
- **Contact**: an enquiry form, WhatsApp and what happens after you get in touch

The site is designed mobile-first for people on mid-range Android phones and mobile data, with M-Pesa, SMS and WhatsApp at its centre.

## Built with

Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
